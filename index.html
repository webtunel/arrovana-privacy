Arrovana Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026

Arrovana: Arrow Puzzle is provided by MILOŠ CVIJIĆ PR WEBILLIUM, Stapar, Serbia. Contact: office@webillium.com.

Your game progress

The game stores puzzle progress, coins, hints, cosmetic choices, preferences, daily rewards and personal records on your device. No player account is required. We do not operate a cloud save service or public leaderboard. Clearing the app's storage removes these local records; uninstalling normally removes app data. Your Android backup settings may affect restoration.

Advertising and consent

Arrovana uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Advertisements may appear between campaign puzzles. Rewarded advertisements are optional. Where required, the consent form explains your advertising choices before ads are requested. You can reopen available controls through Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Google's advertising SDK collects and shares device IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit. The exact information processed depends on your choices and SDK configuration.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps. You can manage or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Consent choices do not remove all processing needed to provide permitted ads or prevent fraud.

Other information

The release game has no separate developer-operated analytics service. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use it to handle your request. Do not send passwords or financial details.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local progress remains until app data is cleared. Support correspondence is kept while necessary to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Google's retention and international processing practices are described in its policies. Contact us to ask about information you have provided directly, request access, correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. We do not have access to delete locally stored progress remotely.

Audience

Arrovana is intended for a general teen and adult audience, not specifically for children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies this version.
